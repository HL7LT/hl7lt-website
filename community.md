# Community

The **HL7 Lithuania Community** unites healthcare professionals, developers, and
institutions working together to advance digital health interoperability using the
HL7 FHIR® standard.

We believe in open collaboration, transparency, and shared progress — everything we
build is open source and community-driven.

---

## Our Mission

> To support the adoption and implementation of HL7 FHIR® standards in Lithuania through: {.is-success}
>
> - 🧩 Open collaboration and transparent publication
> - 📘 National FHIR Implementation Guides
> - 🌍 Alignment with European interoperability initiatives
> - 🎓 Knowledge sharing and education

---

## How to Contribute

- ### Contribute on GitHub
  Suggest improvements, fix issues, or help shape new Implementation Guides and terminology resources.
  [View Repositories](https://github.com/HL7LT){.button}
- ### Join the Discussions
  Participate in HL7 Lithuania community discussions, propose ideas, and connect with other implementers.
  [Join Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/HL7LT/discussions){.button}
- ### Contact the Team
  Have questions or ideas? Reach out to the HL7 Lithuania coordination team directly.
  [Contact Us](mailto:info@hl7.lt){.button}
{.card-grid}

## Open Source Philosophy

> All HL7 Lithuania Implementation Guides, terminology resources, and documentation are developed in the open.
>
> Anyone can reuse, adapt, or contribute to them under permissive open-source licenses.
>
> Transparency and collaboration are essential to building trustworthy national digital health standards.
